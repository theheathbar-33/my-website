My perspective on AI-era content marketing

Content Quality Over Volume

AI has lowered the cost of content creation — which means quantity alone is no longer a competitive advantage. The companies that win will create the most useful, trustworthy, and discoverable content, not the most content.

Human Expertise Remains the Differentiator

AI can draft, summarize, and accelerate — but it cannot replace subject matter expertise, customer empathy, or strategic judgment. The most effective content is still rooted in human insight and narratives that build trust.

SEO and AEO Are Converging

Buyers increasingly discover information through AI-powered search, answer engines, and conversational interfaces. Content must be designed for both SEO and AEO — authoritative, structured, and optimized to surface wherever buyers seek answers.

AI-Assisted Workflows Create Leverage

I view AI as a force multiplier. Used thoughtfully, it accelerates research, streamlines production, improves repurposing, and reduces bottlenecks — freeing teams to spend more time on strategy, storytelling, and optimization.

Content Is a Growth Engine

The highest-performing content programs are not publishing functions — they are growth functions. When aligned with business objectives, content becomes a scalable asset that drives organic visibility, generates demand, and compounds in value over time.
