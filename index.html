My perspective on AI-era content marketing

The companies that win won't produce the most content. They'll produce the most useful content.

AI has dramatically lowered the cost of content creation — which means quantity alone is no longer a competitive advantage. The challenge now is creating content that demonstrates expertise, answers real customer questions, and provides value that generic AI-generated content cannot. I view AI as a force multiplier: used thoughtfully, it eliminates repetitive tasks so teams can focus on strategy, storytelling, and optimization.
