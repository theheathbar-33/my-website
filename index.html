My perspective on AI-era content marketing

The companies that win won't produce the most content — they'll produce the most useful content.

Here's the uncomfortable truth about AI and content: anyone can now produce more. That means volume is no longer the edge. The companies winning right now are the ones creating content that's genuinely useful, clearly expert, and structured to be found — by both humans and AI answer engines. I use AI to do more of the strategic work, not less of it.
